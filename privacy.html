Who we are

Out of Office coworking, Tsimiski 56, Thessaloniki, Greece. Contact: hello@outofoffice.gr · +30 2310 235 892. We are responsible for the personal data you give us when you book a desk.

What we collect

  • Your name, email, phone number and address
  • Company name and VAT number, if you give them
  • Any comments you write
  • The desk and days you book

Why we use it

Only to manage your booking, contact you about it, and issue invoices. The legal basis is that we need this data to provide the booking you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for invoices, our legal accounting obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). We do not use your data for marketing and we never sell or share it.

Where it is stored

Bookings are saved in our Google account (Google Sheets and Gmail). Google may process data outside the EU under its own data protection terms. The booking page is hosted by GitHub, which keeps standard server logs such as IP addresses. The page uses no cookies and no tracking.

How long we keep it

We delete booking details 24 months after your last booking. Invoice details are kept for as long as Greek tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to limit how we use it, or to receive a copy. Just email hello@outofoffice.gr. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).
